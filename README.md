# 42 (*, 0x2A, 101010): The meaning of life, the universe, and everything
## Opening letter from a past-pisciner:
The piscine is scary. Very scary. You're being asked to keep working on projects with totally new concepts and increasing difficulty.
Look, you might feel like you are uncapable of doing it, but at the end of the piscine there's only one type of person that passes. The person that wanted to pass to begin with.  
It's not about how smart you are, how much you know about programming, how good at math you are... The piscine is not about any of those things.  
Hell, it's not even about how good you are at asking others for help! (Although mutual help between peers is going to be essential!)
No. From the very beginning of the piscine, you are the one who determines if you're going to pass the final exam and succeed. Look around you, everyone's lost just like you, learning from scratch.  
And that's exactly what the piscine is about: **The ability to figure things out yourself, and MAKING SHIT HAPPEN.**  
No matter how dumb or lost you feel right now, if you know you truly want to succeed, trust me, you and anyone around you, can make it happen.

## About the repository
To me, this repository is going to be like a time capsule. Something I can come back to and cringe at, hopefully, because I think that'd mean I got significantly better at writing code.  
To you, I hope this repository helps you move on if you get stuck, helps you clarify doubts on things you might be struggling to wrap your head around, and more.  
I'm not including the Shell00 and Shell01 projects here because the initial learning phase is going to be critical, you need to figure these commands out yourself and become better at using the terminal.
